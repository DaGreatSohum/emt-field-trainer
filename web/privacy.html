Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Training data

EMT Field Trainer sends scenario responses, progress, scores, and technical attempt identifiers to its backend so the training simulation can operate. Do not enter real patient names, protected health information, or other sensitive personal information.

Use and sharing

Training data is used only to provide and improve app functionality. The app does not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking and does not sell it.

Retention and security

Attempts are configured for deletion after the operator's retention period, normally 90 days. Data is protected in transit with HTTPS in production, and attempts use scoped access tokens.

Your choices

The app does not require an account. Contact the app operator through the support URL listed on the App Store product page for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Educational use

This app is a training aid, not clinical decision support. Follow current protocols, local policy, medical direction, and authorized scope in actual patient care.
